Our Programmes

Our programmes are designed to provide practical support and opportunities that can help children learn, grow and develop.

Education Support

Our education programme focuses on helping children access learning resources and opportunities that support their academic development.

The programme can include educational materials, learning activities and community-based support.

Nutrition Support

Our nutrition programme aims to promote children's wellbeing by supporting access to nutritious food and basic nutritional assistance.

Healthy nutrition can help children stay active, focused and ready to learn.

Community Support

Our community support programme encourages people to work together to create positive opportunities for children.

Volunteers can contribute their time, skills and resources to support community activities.
