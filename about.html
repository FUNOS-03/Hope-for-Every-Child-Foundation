About Us

Hope for Every Child Foundation is a non-profit organisation committed to improving the lives of vulnerable children and families through education, nutrition and community support.

We believe every child deserves an opportunity to grow, learn and build a better future, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Our Mission

Our Purpose

Our mission is to support vulnerable children and families by providing access to educational opportunities, nutritional assistance and community-based support.

Our Vision

Our vision is a society where every child has the opportunity to reach their full potential and enjoy a safe, healthy and hopeful future.

Our Values

We value compassion, dignity, accountability, equality and teamwork. These values guide our commitment to children and their communities.

Who We Support

We aim to assist vulnerable children, disadvantaged families and communities that need additional support. We also welcome volunteers, sponsors and organisations that share our commitment to making a positive difference.
